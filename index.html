CryptoData is a desktop utility being configured to back up CryptoData research files to the owner's Google Drive account.

What the application does

CryptoData collects public cryptocurrency market data for research. The backup utility is intended to upload recovery archives containing the research database, program source, and selected configuration files to Google Drive. It does not place trades or provide investment advice.

Why Google access is requested

Google OAuth connects the locally running utility to Google Drive without asking the application to handle your Google password. The intended permission is drive.file: access to files created by this app or explicitly selected for use with it. It does not request access to your entire Drive.

Google Drive integration requires the account owner's consent. This website is informational; it does not collect OAuth credentials or run the backup process.

Control stays with the account owner

You can stop the local backup process, delete its Drive backups, and revoke Google access at any time. Read the privacy policy for data use, retention, and deletion details.
