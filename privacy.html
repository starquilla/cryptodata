Privacy and your backups.

This policy describes the intended Google Drive integration for CryptoData Backup, a personal desktop research backup utility maintained by starquilla.

Data accessed and purpose

With your authorization, the utility uses the Google Drive API to upload and manage its research backup files. Backups may contain a market-research database, program source, selected configuration, and recovery documentation. Drive file identifiers, names, sizes, and upload status may be processed to manage and verify backups.

The integration is designed to request only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, covering files created by the app or explicitly selected for use with it. It does not request Gmail, contacts, or unrestricted access to all Drive files.

OAuth credentials and storage

Google handles sign-in and consent. The application does not receive your Google password. OAuth access and refresh tokens are intended to be stored on the computer running the utility and used only to maintain authorized Drive access. Credentials and tokens must be excluded from backup archives, public repositories, and this website.

Research data remains on the owner's computer and in backups uploaded to the owner's Google Drive. Local operational records may contain backup metadata and errors.

Sharing and use limitations

Google receives backup contents and API requests as the storage provider. The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. It does not intentionally share backups with other users. The owner's Google Drive sharing settings control any additional access.

CryptoData Backup's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

Drive backups remain until the owner deletes them or an explicitly configured retention process removes them. Local files and operational records remain until removed on the owner's computer. No automatic Drive retention period is currently configured.

You can revoke access through Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorized requests but does not delete existing backups. To remove stored data, stop the local utility, delete its local token files and unwanted local data, and delete the backup files from Drive, including Trash when permanent removal is desired.

This website

These static pages contain no login form, analytics scripts, or application tracking cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process technical request information under GitHub's privacy statement. Google processes data under its own applicable policies.
